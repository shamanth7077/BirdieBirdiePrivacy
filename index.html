Privacy Policy for Birdie Birdie

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Birdie Birdie ("the app," "we," "us") is developed by an independent developer. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and your rights regarding that data.

Data We Collect

Birdie Birdie itself does not require an account, login, or any personal information like your name, email, or phone number to play. The app uses two third-party services that collect limited technical data automatically:

GameAnalytics (game analytics) collects:

  • A device identifier and an internal user identifier (not your name or email)
  • How you interact with the app — level progress, ad interactions, purchase events
  • Crash and performance data, to help us fix bugs and improve stability

Google AdMob (advertising) collects:

  • Device and advertising-related technical data, used to show interstitial and rewarded video ads
  • We do not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, and ads are shown in non-personalized mode — we do not track you across other apps or websites

If you purchase "Remove Ads" (available once released), that transaction is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play — we never see your payment details.

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, physical address, health data, financial information, precise or approximate location, contacts, or any user-generated content. The app has no chat, messaging, or social features of any kind.

How We Use This Data

Collected data is used only to: understand how the app is used so we can improve it, display and measure advertising, and diagnose crashes and performance issues. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone beyond the service providers listed below, who process it on our behalf.

Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • GameAnalytics — gameanalytics.com/privacy-policy
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google User Messaging Platform (consent management) — policies.google.com/privacy

Your Choices

If you're in the EU, UK, or another region requiring consent for advertising, you'll see a consent prompt on first launch. You can change your choice anytime via Settings → Privacy Options in the app.

Since the app doesn't require an account, there's no personal profile to access, export, or delete beyond uninstalling the app, which removes all locally saved data (your progress and settings) from your device immediately.

Children's Privacy

Birdie Birdie is not directed at children and is not listed in a children's category on the App Store or Google Play. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your region). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the email below and we'll address it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

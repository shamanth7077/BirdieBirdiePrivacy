Support for Birdie Birdie

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Birdie Birdie is developed by an independent developer. If you're running into a problem, have feedback, or just want to say hi, this page is the fastest way to reach us.

Contact

Email us at aitrendsdaily8888@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can. Please include your device model and iOS/Android version if you're reporting a bug — it helps us track things down faster.

Frequently Asked Questions

I ran out of lives — what now?

Lives regenerate over time on their own, or you can watch a short rewarded ad for an extra life right from the "Out of Lives" screen.

I bought "Remove Ads" but I'm still seeing ads / reinstalled the app.

Open Settings inside the app and tap "Restore Purchases." This re-links your purchase to your Apple ID or Google account without charging you again.

The app crashed or a level isn't working correctly.

Email us at the address above with a short description of what happened, your device model, and OS version — screenshots or a screen recording help a lot too.

How do I delete my data?

Birdie Birdie doesn't require an account, so there's no server-side profile to delete. Uninstalling the app removes all locally saved progress and settings from your device immediately.
